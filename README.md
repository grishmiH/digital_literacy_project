PROJECT OVERVIEW:

This repository contains my Digital Literacy Project completed as part of the Digital Literacy(CSE0001) course. In this project, I have taken the role of a Student Digital Ambassador and created a portfolio to help understand and promote digital skills among students.

The project focuses on important aspects such as digital awareness, online safety, professional presence, digital communication etiquette, and cybercrime prevention. Each task helped me explore different tools and platforms that are useful for both academic and professional growth.



TASK SUMMARY:

Task 1: Digital Literacy Infographic

I created an infographic using Canva to explain the concept of digital literacy and its importance. The design includes topics like safe internet practices, useful tools, and maintaining a professional online presence.

Task 2: Digital Portfolio

I created and updated my profiles on platforms like GitHub, Kaggle and LinkedIn. This helped me understand how to build a professional digital presence.

Task 3: Platforms Exploration

I practised coding using HackerRank by solving a beginner-level problem. I also created a Digital Literacy Awareness Quiz using Google Forms to collect responses and understand how data can be analyzed.

Google form: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeGRW6L2f8-IS61RmkSzxLF40lOJ2cCqB6CI6H7ugbLVA3Z4Q/viewform?usp=publish-editor

Task 4: Email Etiquette

I drafted two professional emails—one for requesting an assignment extension and another for applying for an internship. I also created a checklist for responsible social media usage.

Task 5: Cybercrime Awareness

I worked on a case study related to cybercrime and created a prevention checklist to spread awareness about online safety, including tips for UPI security and reporting cybercrime.
