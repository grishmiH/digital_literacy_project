SOCIAL MEDIA CHECKLIST:

> DO'S:
1. Use strong and unique passwords for all accounts.
2. Keep your profile professional and respectful.
3. Think twice before posting anything online.
4. Verify information before sharing it.
5. Be respectful and polite in comments and messages.
6. Adjust privacy settings to control who sees your content.
7. Use social media for learning and networking.(e.g., LinkedIn, Kaggle, Github)

>DON'TS:
1. Don’t share personal details like phone number, address, or OTP.
2. Don’t post offensive, abusive, or inappropriate content.
3. Don’t spread fake news or unverified information.
4. Don’t click on suspicious links or unknown messages.
5. Don’t overshare your location or daily routine.
6. Don’t engage in online arguments or cyberbullying.
7. Don’t post anything that could harm your future career.
