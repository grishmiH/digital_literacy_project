Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist

General Safety Tips:
- Use strong and unique passwords for all your accounts.
- Enable two-factor authentication wherever possible.
- Do not click on unknown or suspicious links.
- Always verify the source before sharing any information.
- Keep your devices and apps updated regularly.
- Avoid using public Wi-Fi for sensitive activities.

UPI & Financial Safety:
- Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, or bank details with anyone.
- Always double-check the receiver’s details before making payments.
- Do not accept unknown payment requests.


 Reporting Cybercrime:
- Report any suspicious activity at cybercrime.gov.in
- Call the Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930 for immediate help.
